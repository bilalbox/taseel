# UI prototypes (three paradigms)

Switch variants via the bottom pill bar, `←`/`→`, or `?variant=A|B|C`.

## A — Split-pane manuscript reader (`?variant=A`)

Classical study layout: Amiri body text with highlighted fiqh/usul principles, timed audio from the shaykh (OPFS/`opfs-sahpool`, no COEP/COOP), side margin with principle definition and takhrij rationale, and an inline quiz with distractors.

## B — Keyboard-first active recall (`?variant=B`)

One focused card (question first). Keys `[1]`–`[4]` pick the principle; `Space`/`Enter` advances after the audio rationale; `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` opens command-palette search over principles. Shows speed, streak, and XP.

## C — Principle tree & comparison matrix (`?variant=C`)

Hierarchical navigation across major fiqh maxims, usul principles, and chapter-scoped dawabit, plus a side-by-side Ibn Baz / Ibn Uthaymeen takhrij matrix and per-principle mastery bars.
